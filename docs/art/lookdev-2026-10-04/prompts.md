## A: initial art direction

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: standalone look-development concept for ZipperCrew's Corner Café, Direction A — Modern stylized 3D. A finished wide 16:9 game-scene still, NOT a UI mockup. No text, labels, logos, borders or interface.

Implementer: energetic inventive young adult, lean springy silhouette, dramatic swept auburn forelock with closely cropped sides, distinctive angular coral headphones, terracotta cropped technical jacket over off-white shirt, charcoal tapered utility trousers, bold cream sneakers. Sharp expressive eyebrows, confident asymmetrical concentration. Seated leaning forward at a modern keyboard, hands visibly on the keys; monitor does not hide face.
Reviewer: composed analytical dark-skinned adult woman, sculptural asymmetric silver wedge bob, thin angular dark spectacles, striking broad-shouldered cropped teal jacket, dark tailored wide trousers, clean dark shoes with small teal detail. Taller solid trapezoidal silhouette, slightly skeptical eyebrow, measured posture, standing beside her workstation inspecting a thin tablet with stylus. Human: calm authoritative medium-skinned adult woman, bold asymmetrical indigo curly updo held with a geometric copper clip, structured plum overshirt with rolled cuffs, pale stone wide trousers, dark minimalist sneakers. Distinct rounded hair silhouette over strong relaxed stance. In the foreground beside the small shared table, holding a phone at waist height, observing the crew with quiet confidence, not a corporate manager.

One architectural plant maximum. Fewer objects, no ornamental shelves. Warmth comes from light and wood, not vintage décor.
Light/materials: decisive warm side key from window, cool restrained fill, rich occlusion and dark cast-shadow masses, crisp specular accents on glass and coated metal, matte technical fabric, smooth sculpted hair masses. Clear lit/shadow separation, no haze, no bloom. Modern game-ready forms, not photorealistic humans, not toy figurines.
Avoid: painterly brushwork, storybook/watercolor, rustic/vintage café, cottagecore, beige-green wash, uniform soft light, all-rounded furniture, clutter, excessive plants, chibi, generic office people, children's film look, 2010s casual mobile game look, recognizable existing game characters or imitation of any franchise.
