Sheet title (verbatim): "A — UNRULY".
This is the most playful, irregular, surprising direction, blunt and wonky rather than elegant.
HUMAN: Jacob-Collier-inspired warm mischievous inhabitant. Huge irregular fan of dark unruly hair with 5 broad lopsided clumps; broad peach face, prominent friendly eyebrows, simplified angular nose, asymmetrical crooked grin. Head/hair over half total height. Small yellow/orange irregular striped sleeveless knit over cream short-sleeved shirt, roomy purple patch trousers compressed into two short stumps, mismatched simple red and turquoise shoes. No thin elegant torso. Neutral pose hands loose, curious face. Characteristic pose: arms conducting outward at DIFFERENT heights, mitten fingers, lively open grin, one foot slightly lifted. Clothing is 3–4 large blocks only.
IMPLEMENTER: a raspberry-red peculiar two-eared bean creature, broad floppy squared-off head merging into very squat body, one triangular ear up and one floppy ear sideways, face high in body, wide eager mouth, small bright dot eyes, indigo single broad overall bib, two short mitten arms and little black boot feet. Off-kilter silhouette with asymmetrical ear outline, emphatically not human or robot. Neutral alert leaning slightly forward. Characteristic pose: delighted springy crouch holding up a tiny hand-tool and a plain work slip, eager ready-to-make gesture; no drawn motion effects.
REVIEWER: a sentient pale-mint folded paper inhabitant, upright narrow folded-sheet body with one diagonally folded upper corner forming a skeptical brow, dark simple eyes, one brow lifted, tiny calm mouth. Its papery head/body one unit, visibly 2D accordion lower edge, thin short plum arms, tiny black feet, one large plum side tab. No human clothes, no sleek robot. Neutral standing with arms tucked calmly. Characteristic pose: holds a small cream review slip near its face and points precisely to one mark, head slightly tilted skeptically.
The three silhouettes must be strikingly different: unruly big-haired human, wide floppy-eared red bean, upright angular folded-paper being.

Sheet title (verbatim): "B — BOLD GRAPHIC".
Most minimal direction: only a handful of chunky shapes per inhabitant, strong flat-color areas, irregular outlines, dry wit. Graphic simplification, not an elegant brand illustration.
HUMAN: Jacob-Collier-inspired face as a wide off-square peach block beneath a tall uneven 4-spike black hair mass, huge face and hair account for 55 percent overall height. Wide bold brows, nose a single small angle, friendly sideways grin. Tiny boxy forest-teal top with ONE coral patch on one sleeve and ONE yellow block across lower torso, stubby brick-red trouser legs, two different-colored little shoes. No intricate print. Neutral pose hands low. Characteristic pose: one mitten hand near head, other conducting outward as if juggling ideas, raised delighted brow, tipped head. Warm eccentric creative presence.
IMPLEMENTER: an anthropomorphic mustard-orange clamp/tool creature. Tallish asymmetric open clamp-shaped head with one large hooked side and one shorter straight side, very simple dark face on the main central block, coral compact body, two stubby uneven arms and two flat boots. Made of bold flat drawn areas, deliberately odd hand tool inhabitant, no chrome, no gradients, no polished friendly robot box. Neutral upright energetic face. Characteristic pose: head-clamp tilts eagerly toward a tiny work slip held forward, free hand giving a brisk maker's gesture.
REVIEWER: a broad low aubergine-purple pebble creature whose horizontally stretched flat oval head/body sits on two very tiny rust feet. Extremely wide compared with height, thick deadpan eyebrows, mismatched small dot eyes, small straight mouth. One small flat cream patch on underside, two thin very short arms. NO ears, hair or costume, no glossy rounded surface, just flat outlined funny oval. Neutral calm skeptical arms folded. Characteristic pose: one tiny hand lifts a cream document, other finger points to a line, one eyebrow raised.
Do not force the characters to share a template. Silhouettes: spiky square-headed tiny human, tall irregular open-hook tool-being, ultra-wide low pebble. Flat cut-paper boldness with slightly imperfect dark contours. Limited teal, coral, mustard, aubergine; avoid reference palette.

Strong straight eyebrows, simple open warm smile and visible playful nose angle. Head and hair 55 percent height; almost no neck. Tiny loose shirt with three large unusual mustard, plum and cream color-blocks, two short navy trouser stumps, small mismatched ochre and green shoes. Neutral relaxed pose curious half-smile. Absolutely no sleek industrial design, antenna bulb, shiny surface, 3D rendering or corporate mascot symmetry. Neutral eager forward stance. Characteristic pose: holding a small plain finished work slip aloft while free hand still grips a stubby pencil, proud energetic smile.
REVIEWER: a peculiar calm bird-like creature with a huge broad sage-green angular-dome head, tiny cream beak off center, heavy observant asymmetric dark brows, small dot eyes, very small cream lower body in rust waistcoat, two short flat black feet. Neutral still watchful face, tiny wings loosely at sides. Characteristic pose: a small cream review slip raised in one mitten-like wing, the other tucked to chin with dry skeptical curiosity.
Silhouettes must remain distinct: tufted pear-headed human, crank-eared wide-box handmade robot, wide plumed bird head over tiny waistcoat body. Flat sage/ochre/rust/plum. No shading whatsoever.
